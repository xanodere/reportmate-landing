AgencyAnalytics vs ReportMate: Which is Better for Small Agencies?

ReportMate Blog · May 2026

AgencyAnalytics: powerful but complex

AgencyAnalytics is the market leader for agency reporting dashboards. It connects to 80+ platforms, has custom dashboards, and is used by thousands of agencies worldwide.

But for small agencies (under 15 clients), it can be overkill:

  • Starts at $15/month for 5 clients, but quickly reaches $200+/month for growing agencies
  • Setup requires configuring dashboards for each client — significant upfront time
  • Clients get a link to a dashboard, not a PDF in their inbox
  • Many clients don't log in — your reports go unread

ReportMate: built for PDF delivery

ReportMate is purpose-built for one thing: automated PDF client reports delivered to inboxes.

  • Connect GA4, Meta Ads, Google Ads via OAuth (2 min per client)
  • Reports generate and send automatically every month
  • Branded PDFs with your agency's logo and colors
  • Starts at €29/month for up to 3 clients

Which should you use?

Choose AgencyAnalytics if: You have 20+ clients, need 30+ integrations, and want interactive dashboards clients can explore.
