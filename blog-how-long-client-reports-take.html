How Long Do Client Reports Take? (Agency Survey)

ReportMate Blog · May 2026

The average marketing agency spends 27 hours/month on client reports

We asked 50 marketing agencies and freelancers how long they spend building monthly client reports. The results were striking:

  • Agencies with 1-5 clients: 8-15 hours/month
  • Agencies with 5-15 clients: 20-35 hours/month
  • Agencies with 15+ clients: 40-80 hours/month

The biggest time sinks, in order:

  1. Exporting data from GA4, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads (40% of time)
  2. Formatting data into a readable document (35% of time)
  3. Creating PDF and branding it with the agency's look (15% of time)
  4. Emailing it to clients (10% of time)

Why agencies haven't automated this yet

The most common answer: "The tools that exist are either too expensive, too complex, or don't send PDFs — they just create dashboards clients don't look at."

Most agencies want one thing: a branded PDF in the client's inbox every month. Not a link to a dashboard. Not a login screen. A PDF.

How to cut reporting time to near zero

The agencies that spend the least time on reports have one thing in common: they've connected directly to the platform APIs (GA4, Meta, Google Ads) and set up automated PDF generation and delivery.
