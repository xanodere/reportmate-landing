How to Create a GA4 Report for Clients (Without Spending Hours)

ReportMate Blog · May 2026

The manual way (what most agencies still do)

  1. Log into GA4 for the client's property
  2. Set date range to last month
  3. Screenshot or export: sessions, users, top pages, channels
  4. Paste into a Google Doc or PowerPoint
  5. Format, brand it with client colors, add commentary
  6. Export to PDF
  7. Email to client

Average time: 45-90 minutes per client. For 10 clients: a full day of work, every month.

What a good GA4 client report should include

  • Sessions and Users — with % change vs. previous period
  • New vs. returning users
  • Top 5-10 pages by views
  • Traffic by channel (Organic, Paid, Social, Direct)
  • Avg. session duration and bounce rate
  • Goal completions / conversions (if set up)

The automated way
